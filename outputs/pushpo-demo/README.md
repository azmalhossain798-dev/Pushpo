# Pushpo — The Golden Return

A buildless browser demo for desktop and mobile. Run `node serve.mjs`, then open http://127.0.0.1:4173. The deployable application is in `dist/`.

For the easiest start, open the supplied `pushpo-play.html` in Chrome or Edge. It embeds all game code and artwork and requires no server, installation, or internet connection. Refresh or reopen an older copy after updating.

## Play

- WASD or arrow keys: move. On touch screens use the left joystick.
- Q / Slap: open-hand strike with an 0.19-second contact delay (18 damage).
- Space, canvas click / Punch & Kick: one accepted press alternates punch and kick (35 damage each). Punch contacts at 0.17 seconds; kick at 0.28 seconds with slightly longer reach. Cooldown clicks do not advance the combo.
- E / Sight: unlock after five enemy defeats, then reveal nearby enemies and civilians. Revealed targets stay identified.
- R / Water Mirror: unlock after Sumon. Auto-react to a connecting shot, weapon swing or grab, or cast manually. A translucent full-body water bubble protects for exactly 3 seconds; all activations share a 10-second cooldown.
- Shift / Dodge: dash away from a telegraphed attack.
- F / Eat fuchka: approach a vendor and press F or tap the contextual button.
- Esc / Pause: pause and resume. Switching browser tabs automatically pauses.
- M / minimap: open the district map and pause.

A run begins with 100 health, 0 points, and 15 minutes. Murderers have 100 health and award 10 points; rapists have 150 health and award 15 points; snatchers have 70 health and award 5 points. There are at most four regular enemies, with at most two attacking at once. Killing enemies never restores health. Ordinary civilians cannot be attacked. Two special civilian taunters (rounded 1% of 180) allow one nonlethal slap after speaking; they flee without awarding points or kills. Rapists use ordinary pedestrian artwork, blue civilian markers and civilian wandering behavior until Sight identifies them; they cannot automatically reveal themselves by attacking. All enemy labels, auras, map dots, targeting arrows and boss health displays require unlocked, active, in-range Sight; identities conceal again when it expires. Known/aggro metadata does not reveal identity. Opening attackers remain fightable before the fifth defeat.

Only Slap and Punch & Kick appear in the starting combat pad; movement, Dodge and utilities remain. At 12 seconds a marker guides the player toward a nearby snatcher. Later tutorial ambushes use existing enemy actors. The opening enemy pool uses snatchers/murderers until the fifth defeat, avoiding a locked-Sight progression deadlock; post-unlock replenishment introduces a disguised rapist. Sight and Mirror unlock announcements are separate from ambient dialogue.

Melee damage occurs once at animation contact, rechecking the locked target, range, visibility and walls. Nonlethal hits cause stagger/recoil and interrupt windups. Snatchers have a 38% chance to flee after each surviving hit until their first flight; fleeing is not a kill. They stop attacking, use road/detour navigation, and resume wandering with a combat cooldown. Their original attack dialogue remains alongside two randomized escape lines. Murderers fire aimed, telegraphed shots and retain one assigned Bengali line, spoken once per encounter. A new encounter requires eight seconds beyond 650 world units. Exact dialogue and action timings live in `dist/combat-v7.mjs`.

Sumon Mia unlocks at 150 points (450 health). After his defeat, read the Bengali verdict and Pushpo's reflection, then earn 150 additional combat points to reveal Hafizur Rahman (600 health). The HUD tracks those new points; reading notes never awards points. Defeat these bosses before their active hunt countdown expires. Sumon starts the next 15-minute hunt without healing. Hafizur's existing verdict leads to a safe interlude: Hercules emerges from a connected street corner and approaches before a reader-paced Bengali conversation. All seven requested turns are preserved across 12 paragraph-sized cards. Cards and the approach suspend combat and countdown; Continue, Enter or Space advances text.

The unknown fish gives Pushpo part of its own life force for only 60 days. She wakes alone in an apartment and recalls their conversation before choosing to hunt offenders. This is a narrative lifespan shown in the opening and HUD, not a simulated calendar; it does not replace the active 15-minute hunt clock. The opening button is Get Revenge. Pushpo remains an adult, age 18; there is no assault imagery or graphic gore.

After the Hercules introduction, the Sohel lead is recorded and exploration/combat remain available. Hercules patrols and hunts separate rapist targets using the shared road, collision, fleeing and contact-combat systems. His kills leave the exact pocket note in `story-v8.mjs` and appear in the Pocket notes journal; they grant Pushpo no health, points, kills or ability credit. His targets remain normally hittable after Sight if Pushpo intervenes; rewards follow the attacker. The journal pauses the world. Hercules never follows Pushpo's position as his patrol objective.

After at least 30 seconds of investigation and a completed independent hunt (or a 90-second fallback), Hercules follows the lead physically to East Junction roundabout. His arrival starts one 60-second rescue countdown and spawns Sohel (300 health, small knife) with two companions (200 health each, long worn machetes). Reach the marked roundabout on foot before the deadline; arrival latches and leaving never restarts the clock. Missing the deadline kills Hercules and ends the run. The interlude and final fight have no additional 15-minute countdown.

Hercules uses alternating punches/kicks, target switching, guarding, evasive movement and real health damage. He enters the outnumbered encounter with 360 health and fights defensively until rescued. His area freeze holds nearby enemies for three seconds, with a 12-second cooldown shown beside his health. Enemy weapons keep their locked target through contact and can miss or be blocked; Pushpo retains Water Mirror. Both allies must survive. Defeat all three enemies, in any order, to begin eight reader-paced Bengali farewell cards; victory appears only after the last farewell is dismissed. The ending acknowledges her few remaining borrowed days and Hercules’s promise to protect innocents. No later fight starts. Restart resets all mission flags, clocks, notes, powers and the randomized city. Final balance, dialogue and state transitions live in `dist/final-v9.mjs`.

## Fuchka and daylight

Three fuchka shopkeepers serve Pushpo’s favourite food at Mirpur Road, Boundary Road and Kathaltola. Green map dots mark unused stalls; used stalls turn gray. Each shopkeeper serves one healing meal per run, independently of the others. Meals restore 20% of maximum health (20 at the default 100 maximum), capped at full health; the feedback shows the actual recovery. Full health does not consume the meal. The prompt displays already-used/full-health states and is unavailable during attacks, dodges, pause, story or cinematics. Food grants no points, kills or invulnerability, and never changes mission timers. Day/night changes and chapter transitions do not reset shop usage.

Day and night repeat in 210-second phases, with 24-second dusk/dawn fades. Sky/background tint, ambient color, sunlight and shadow direction change; existing streetlamps and stalls gain warm light pools at night. Combat effects and mission guidance stay above the lighting pass. The cycle follows existing elapsed game time, pauses with menus/story, and stays independent of hunt/rescue countdowns and the narrative lifespan. There is no new weather system, clock, ticker or calendar.

The population remains 180: three existing roaming residents become additional anchored vendors, preserving the ten original activity NPCs, nine home visitors, homeless resident and special interactions. Exactly three recognizable counters use one baked canopy, glass food-display and utensil texture; each retained seller has a distinct apron and a permanent stall binding. Reinitialization repairs duplicate or obsolete seller roles without duplicating shops or resetting usage. Their carts are outside driving lanes and approach points remain walkable. Extra bottles, papers, wrappers, cartons, newspapers and crushed cans are seeded along curbs and baked once into the existing ground texture (761 pieces for the current fixed road layout); litter has no colliders or runtime spawns. Night pools are capped at 24 visible sources. Device frame rate still requires browser/hardware verification.

No accounts, analytics, saved personal data, server state, external fonts, or runtime CDN requests. Audio is synthesized locally after the sound control is enabled.

From the workspace root, regenerate the portable edition with `node work/export-demo.mjs` before running its tests. Run `node work/check-pixi-mutations.mjs` for isolated civilian-protection, countdown and collision mutations. These checks never modify the production simulation.

The main scene uses Pixi sprites, Graphics and Text through a pooled drawing backend. `game-v3.mjs` owns the only game animation loop; Pixi's automatic ticker is disabled. HTML/CSS retains menus, HUD and accessible controls. Canvas 2D remains for one-time texture baking, sprite alpha cropping, label measurements and north-up UI maps. Ground textures are split into tiles no larger than 1024 pixels; DPR is capped at 2.

## Art

Adult character sprites, storefronts, and Pushpo's portrait were generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool, inspired by the supplied visual reference. The mysterious golden fish, known only as Unknown, moves around Pushpo with front/back layering, a waving tail, and a glowing trail. Its nature and reason for choosing Pushpo remain unexplained. Walking, combat, warnings, and environmental details are rendered on canvas.

The expanded 4600 × 4800 district follows the supplied map's main street relationships: Begum Rokeya Sarani, Mirpur Road, the central junction, Boundary Road, northern lanes, and eastern streets. It includes 119 road sections, three roundabouts and 194 buildings, with textured asphalt, warm paved sidewalks, dense storefronts, trees, utility cables, CNGs and market props. Buildings and distances are fictionalized for gameplay. A consistent 2:1 isometric projection aligns roads, sidewalks, ground effects, and world positions. Buildings, trees, vehicles, stalls, and people draw in depth order. Foreground objects fade around Pushpo, the final combat group and nearby interactive stalls. The minimap stays north-up. Utility poles have minimum spacing in world and projected screen coordinates; nearby duplicate lamps are omitted.

Begum Rokeya Sarani and Mirpur Road are 360-unit four-lane boulevards: two 90-unit lanes in each direction, double center lines, dashed lane separators, edge lines, directional arrows and full-width crosswalks. Paint is baked once and omitted at crossing streets and roundabout entries. Rickshaws use the outer lanes; side-road approach distances account for the width of the road being crossed so stopped vehicles do not block through traffic. The central roundabout has a wider, marked two-lane circulating road. Adjacent landmarks, parked props, shopkeepers, house portals and the Mirpur fuchka stall have authored curb positions clear of the wider carriageways. Road names/nodes, all NPC identities/roles, artwork, metro, combat and mission progression are preserved. See `dist/boulevards-v11.mjs` for paint and furniture clearance.

Building sprites are rectified once at startup using calibrated left/front/right ground corners. Both visible facades follow the projected footprint while vertical walls stay upright; smaller lots retain the same vertical storey scale. Landmark footprints clear sidewalks and roads. Roadside bottles, paper, wrappers and cartons are seeded into the baked ground texture, away from driving lanes and junction centers; litter has no collision or gameplay effects.

Fourteen additional rickshaws carry visible drivers and use eight directional views, spinning wheel details and subtle riding motion. They follow left-side lanes, rounded junction turns and roundabout arcs. Junction reservations, safe onward-route selection, braking and shared pedestrian collision checks prevent overlaps; pedestrians can walk around waiting vehicles. Traffic pauses with the game, uses its own seeded random stream, and never damages health. The original parked rickshaws remain scenery.

Pedestrians commit to collision-checked local detours around a rickshaw's entire body instead of repeatedly aiming at its front. Detours account for parked props, retain their waypoints until completed, and replan when blocked. Road/roundabout goals are selected before local avoidance; traffic-occupied intermediate graph nodes can be bypassed. Walkers keep right when passing, maintain body spacing, commit to intermediate road nodes, prefer less crowded onward routes and turn back after five seconds without meaningful progress. Colliders remain active for NPCs and Pushpo. `pedestrian-v5.mjs` implements local detours. `/qa-city.html` includes a stopped-rickshaw crowd reproduction.

One elevated metro line crosses the district. Its reference-based design uses a broad concrete viaduct, flared support piers, overhead catenary, cream-white cars, a green cab, dark windows and red lower trim. Four-car trains arrive after randomized waits of 90–165 seconds, move along the line, stop at Mirpur 10 for six seconds, then leave. The next randomized wait begins after departure. Metro time pauses with the game and resets on restart. The first train is deliberately absent during the first 90 seconds.

All people use a lightweight cutout rig with independent arm/leg swing, weight shift, front/rear artwork, and combat arm strikes. Pushpo's original front-facing artwork and portrait are preserved. The starting population is 180 NPCs (10% fewer), including all ten original activity NPCs, three fuchka vendors assigned from residents, and one roaming homeless resident (approximately 0.56%). Customers step aside and return, vendors gesture, and conversation partners exchange speech bubbles. Nine residents visit three homes: approach, reserve a clear doorway, walk/fade inside, wait and emerge when the exit is clear. Indoor residents remain the same actors but are hidden and excluded from crowd/traffic collisions. The homeless resident occasionally asks a nearby passerby for food or money and resumes roaming. Activity NPCs are not relocated by enemy replenishment; distant new residents use less frequent route updates. Snatchers have three gaunt, worn-clothing variations and a Bengali attack taunt. Revealed rapists deliver their Bengali approach line once per encounter, resetting after eight seconds beyond 650 world units; hidden rapists retain civilian behavior and speech.

Sumon wears a blue shirt and black trousers, Hafizur wears army green, and Sohel is thin and disheveled in black. Hercules uses a generated front/rear atlas based on the attached hood/mask/gloves/boots reference, with a separately animated purple aura. Asset provenance and the exact built-in generation prompt are in `../HERCULES-INTEGRATION.md`. The earlier v1 concept remains separate. The companions use distinct front/rear artwork in `assets/companions-v12.png`: a balding ochre-vest man and a long-haired slate-blue-shirt man. Each long worn machete shares the animated arm’s shoulder and hand transforms; slashes have separate wind-up, contact and recovery, with range/wall/target checks at contact. See `ART-DIRECTION.md` for existing assets.

Active source paths are mapped in the workspace `AGENTS.md`; `homes-v6.mjs` owns home visits and `story-v6.mjs` owns the exact Bengali story beats. `offline.html` is the embedded portable edition. Legacy v1/v2 modules are retained but unused. `qa-world.html` provides all three food interactions, full/used states, day/dusk/night/dawn, clock advance and pause through the actual renderer/engine and shared HUD. `qa-character-motion.html` reviews walking, idle, front/rear, and talking poses. `qa-renderer.html` compares Canvas/Pixi and checks the GPU renderer, pooling and disposal. `qa-responsive.html` resizes the actual game. `qa-final.html` exercises the rescue, failure, freeze, full-health combat and farewell with the actual engine and subtitle presenter. `qa-city.html` provides Sight, combat, homeless, house-visit and metro scenarios plus city checks and a Follow rickshaw control; its time-jump control exists only on this QA page.

GPU antialiasing and text edges may differ slightly from Canvas. The fish and sword use a soft double stroke in place of Canvas shadow blur. The original long-frame behavior is retained: countdown uses elapsed wall time while movement/AI steps are capped at 60 ms. There are no jumping, gravity-inversion or platforming mechanics in Pushpo.
